2026 Market Summary

🏙️ Kathmandu Valley

Strong demand in Ring Road areas. Prices up 8–12% YoY. Baneshwor and Lazimpat remain premium zones. New developments in Budhanilkantha driving growth.

🏔️ Pokhara

Tourism recovery boosting commercial real estate. Lakeside land prices surged 15% post-international airport. Residential demand steady with expat community growing.

📊 Outlook

Infrastructure investments in highways and metro rail expected to drive value in corridor areas. Terai cities showing emerging demand with industrial growth.
